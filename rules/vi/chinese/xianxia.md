# Xianxia / Xuanhuan Genre Rules

## Chinese Source Terms
- 修炼 → tu luyện
- 境界 → cảnh giới
- 灵气 → linh khí
- 丹药 → đan dược
- 法宝 → pháp bảo
- 功法 → công pháp
- 突破 → đột phá
- 渡劫 → độ kiếp
- 内力 → nội lực
- 真气 → chân khí

## Style
- Use an elevated, cultivation-oriented register when the source is solemn, but keep ordinary narration readable
- Keep realm names, techniques, artifacts, and progression terminology exact and consistent
- Convey hierarchy through titles and address choices; do not manufacture archaic dialogue when the source is plain
- In combat and breakthrough scenes, preserve escalation, spatial clarity, and the weight of each power change
