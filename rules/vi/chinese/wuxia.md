# Wuxia Genre Rules

## Chinese Source Terms
- 江湖 → giang hồ
- 武功 → võ công
- 剑法 → kiếm pháp
- 掌门 → chưởng môn
- 门派 → môn phái

## Style
- Preserve the restrained, chivalric atmosphere of giang hồ when present without making every sentence archaic
- Render martial movement with clear, compact verbs and keep action sequences easy to follow
- Keep sect hierarchy, reputation, honor, and obligation visible through tone and forms of address
- Prefer established Hán-Việt martial terminology while keeping surrounding prose natural
