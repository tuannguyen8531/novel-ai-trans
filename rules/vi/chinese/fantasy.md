# Fantasy Genre Rules

## Chinese Source Terms
- 魔法 → ma pháp
- 魔力 → ma lực / mana
- 骑士 → kỵ sĩ
- 魔法师 / 法师 → ma pháp sư / pháp sư
- 冒险者 → mạo hiểm giả
- 公会 → công hội / guild
- 精灵 → tinh linh
- 矮人 → người lùn
- 龙 → rồng

## Style
- Preserve a Western-style fantasy register without importing xianxia or wuxia terminology absent from the source
- Keep magic systems, ranks, races, titles, and institutions terminologically consistent
- Use readable Vietnamese for courtly or medieval dialogue; do not make every character artificially archaic
- Preserve the distinction between native Chinese names and Western-derived in-world names established by the naming policy
