# Fantasy Genre Rules

## Korean Source Terms
- 마법 → ma pháp
- 마력 → ma lực
- 마나 → mana
- 기사 → kỵ sĩ
- 마법사 → ma pháp sư
- 모험가 → mạo hiểm giả
- 길드 → guild
- 엘프 → elf / tinh linh
- 드워프 → người lùn
- 용 → rồng
- 성녀 → thánh nữ

## Style
- Preserve a Western-style fantasy register without forcing Korean contemporary or Murim diction into the setting
- Keep magic systems, ranks, noble titles, races, and institutions terminologically consistent
- Use readable Vietnamese for courtly or medieval dialogue; do not make every character artificially archaic
- Preserve the distinction between Korean names and Western-derived in-world names established by the naming policy
