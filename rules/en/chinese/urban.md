# Urban Genre Rules

## Chinese Source Terms
- 总裁 -> CEO / president / general manager, depending on company context
- 董事长 -> chairman / chairperson
- 经理 -> manager / director, depending on role
- 职场 -> workplace / professional world
- 豪门 -> wealthy elite family / powerful family
- 网红 -> influencer / internet celebrity
- 直播 -> livestream / live broadcast
- 热搜 -> trending search / hot search
- 微信 -> WeChat
- 朋友圈 -> WeChat Moments / social feed
- 小区 -> residential compound / apartment complex

## Style
- Use natural contemporary English and avoid historical, wuxia, or cultivation diction unless deliberately invoked
- Preserve differences between narration, speech, messages, posts, livestreams, and workplace language
- Convey corporate hierarchy, wealth, reputation, family pressure, and social distance through titles and diction without adding explanations
- Localize ordinary urban concepts naturally while keeping culturally important names, institutions, brands, and platforms consistent
