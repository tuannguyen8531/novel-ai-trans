# Academy Genre Rules

## Korean Source Terms
- 아카데미 -> academy
- 생도 -> cadet / student, depending on institution
- 교수 -> professor / instructor
- 수석 / 차석 -> top-ranked student / runner-up
- 입학시험 -> entrance exam
- 실기시험 -> practical exam
- 기숙사 -> dormitory
- 마법학부 -> magic department / faculty of magic
- 검술학부 -> swordsmanship department
- 퇴학 -> expulsion / dismissal

## Style
- Keep academy ranks, classes, departments, training stages, evaluations, and competition terminologically consistent
- Distinguish professors, instructors, students, cadets, seniors, nobles, and commoners through titles and register
- Preserve clarity in practical lessons, duels, and training while retaining ordinary academy life between them
- Do not flatten a fantasy academy into a modern school or make every character artificially archaic
