# Isekai Genre Rules

## Japanese Source Terms
- 異世界 → dị giới
- 召喚 → triệu hồi
- 転生 → chuyển sinh
- 転移 → dịch chuyển / chuyển sang thế giới khác, depending on context

## Style
- Preserve the contrast between a modern protagonist's inner voice and the fantasy world's social register
- Distinguish physical transfer, summoning, and reincarnation instead of flattening them into one event
- Convey discovery, culture shock, and genre humor without adding exposition absent from the source
- Do not assume game systems, modern knowledge, or memories that the source has not established
