# Murim Genre Rules

## Korean Source Terms
- 무림 → Murim / Võ lâm
- 내공 → nội công
- 강호 → giang hồ
- 문파 → môn phái
- 장로 → trưởng lão

## Style
- Use consistent Hán-Việt martial terminology and preserve distinctions between sect, clan, alliance, and school
- Express rank and seniority through titles and address choices rather than explanatory narration
- Keep martial exchanges concise and forceful while preserving tactical detail
- Maintain an elevated but readable giang hồ tone when the source supports it
