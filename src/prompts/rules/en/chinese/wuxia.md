# Wuxia Genre Rules

## Chinese Source Terms
- 江湖 -> jianghu / martial world, depending on glossary and readability
- 武功 -> martial arts / martial skill
- 剑法 -> sword technique / sword art
- 掌门 -> sect leader / clan master
- 门派 -> sect / school / faction
- 轻功 -> qinggong / lightness skill
- 内功 -> internal art / internal cultivation
- 大侠 -> hero / great hero / martial hero

## Style
- Preserve a restrained chivalric and jianghu atmosphere when present without overusing archaic English
- Render martial movement with clear, compact verbs and keep action sequences easy to follow
- Keep sect hierarchy, reputation, honor, and obligation visible through tone and forms of address
- Use established martial terminology consistently while keeping surrounding prose natural
