# Murim Genre Rules

## Korean Source Terms
- 무림 -> Murim / martial world, depending on glossary
- 내공 -> internal energy / internal force
- 강호 -> gangho / martial world, depending on glossary
- 문파 -> sect / school
- 장로 -> elder
- 맹주 -> alliance leader
- 사부 -> master
- 제자 -> disciple

## Style
- Use consistent martial terminology and preserve distinctions between sect, clan, alliance, and school
- Express rank and seniority through titles and address choices rather than explanatory narration
- Keep martial exchanges concise and forceful while preserving tactical detail
- Maintain an elevated but readable martial-world tone when the source supports it
